Project Name: Planora – Event Management System
Website Purpose: A modern platform for seamless event planning, premium templates, collaborative scheduling, and smart event management.

🌈 Color Theme

Planora follows a dark luxury theme with vibrant accents to reflect the energy and elegance of live events.

Core Colors

Primary Background (Dark): #111827 → used for main sections, navbars, and forms.

Secondary Background: #1f2937 → for cards, modals, and content blocks.

Primary Accent (Purple): #c084fc → for buttons, highlights, and key CTAs.

Secondary Accent (Pink): #f472b6 → for links, hover states, and secondary buttons.

Highlight Yellow: #facc15 → sparingly used for emphasis (alerts, important icons).

Text Colors

Primary Text: #ffffff (white, for headers and body on dark backgrounds).

Secondary Text: #d1d5db (light gray, for subtext and form labels).

Muted Text: #9ca3af (placeholders, disabled states).

Link Text / CTA: #ec4899 (pink) or #60a5fa (blue).

Status Colors

Success: #22c55e

Error: #ef4444

Warning: #facc15

Info: #2563eb

🖌️ Design Guidelines

Overall Look & Feel:

Sleek, modern, and premium.

Dark backgrounds with high-contrast typography.

Vibrant accent colors to capture the excitement of events.

Navigation Bar:

Background → #111827.

Text → White (#ffffff).

Active Link Highlight → #c084fc.

Hero Sections:

Use event photography backgrounds with a dark gradient overlay (rgba(0,0,0,0.7)).

Headings in white, CTA buttons in purple accent (#c084fc).

Forms & Cards:

Background → #1f2937.

Rounded corners, soft shadows.

Inputs → Dark background, light borders, placeholder in muted gray.

Buttons:

Primary: Purple background #c084fc, white text. Hover → darker purple #9333ea.

Secondary: Transparent / outlined with purple borders.

Danger: Red (#ef4444) for delete/cancel actions.

Typography:

Headings → Bold, white (#ffffff).

Body text → Light gray (#d1d5db).

Links → Pink (#f472b6) or blue (#60a5fa).

Imagery:

Use event photos with purple/pink neon lighting tones.

Always apply a dark overlay for text readability.

🚀 Tone & Branding

Planora is about elegance, energy, and seamless event planning. The UI must feel professional yet exciting, reflecting live concerts, weddings, and large-scale events
